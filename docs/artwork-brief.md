# Artwork brief

Artwork for Hear Ye, a World of Warcraft: Forever addon that keeps party, guild, nearby and whisper chat in one small window, each conversation in its own row, so players stop missing messages. Everything here is for our artist.

CurseForge requires the logo to be original. It can't use a game's logo or a generic game graphic, or copy another project's logo. The current logo and banner in `art/` are placeholders and will be replaced by this work.

## What's needed

| Asset | Size | Needed for | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project logo | 1024x1024 PNG, square | The CurseForge page and search results | Required |
| In-game icon | The same art, simplified, 512x512 PNG with a transparent background | The AddOns list | Recommended |
| Banner | 1280x400 PNG, with room for the title on the right | The top of the CurseForge description | Nice to have |

Send PNGs at these sizes. They go in the project's `art/` folder as `logo.png`, `icon.png` and `banner.png`, and the in-game icon gets converted to the format and size the game needs.

## Art direction

- **The idea:** the town crier. "Hear ye, hear ye" is what a crier calls out before reading the news in the square. The symbol is the crier's brass hand bell, mid-ring, with a curl of parchment, the message being announced. A bell says "someone is calling for you", which is what the addon is for.
- **The mood:** warm, friendly and a little lively. A busy market square at the end of the day, not an alarm or a warning.
- **The look:** hand-painted fantasy art with chunky shapes and bold, readable silhouettes. Polished brass and cream parchment against a deep slate-blue, with warm lamplight on the bell as the one bright spot. The brass should feel like the bronze borders of the game's own chat and tooltips, so the addon looks like it belongs.
- **Readable when small:** one clear subject, high contrast, and no fine detail that gets lost at icon size. The bell's shape should still read at 64x64 pixels.
- **Fits with our other addons:** Fireside Tales is amber and blue-black, and Tamer is forest green and leather brown, both in the same hand-painted style. Hear Ye should feel like a sibling, in its own brass, parchment and slate blue.

## Things to avoid

- World of Warcraft logos, faction crests, class crests, or text from the game.
- The game's own icons or interface art, such as the chat bubble, the speech icons or the mail icon.
- Blizzard characters, or anything copied from official art.
- Other addons' logos, especially chat addons. A plain speech bubble or a phone-style notification badge would look like every chat app.
- Anything alarming, such as a red alert, a siren or a shouting face. It's an invitation, not an alarm.
- Any text in the art, including words on the parchment. Use squiggle lines for writing if it needs any. The name is added separately.

## Each piece

**Project logo:**

A square icon of a polished brass hand bell with a wooden handle, tilted mid-swing so it's clearly ringing, with a few soft curved lines of sound around it. A curl of cream parchment unrolls behind or beneath it, with squiggle lines for writing but no real text. Framed in a round badge with a worn bronze rim. Deep slate-blue background, warm lamplight glinting on the brass as the one bright spot. High contrast, readable at small sizes. No text, no logos.

**In-game icon:**

The same idea simplified: just the ringing bell and its sound lines, centered on a transparent background. Keep everything well inside a centered circle, because the game crops the corners.

**Banner:**

A wide scene of a small town square at dusk, lanterns lighting up. On the left, a town crier on the steps of a fountain, seen from the side, ringing a brass hand bell high and holding an unrolled parchment. A few townsfolk turn to listen. Keep the crier and the crowd generic, with no faces in focus and no recognizable race armor or characters, and leave open space on the right side for the title. Hand-painted fantasy art in brass, parchment and slate blue, with warm lantern light. No text, no logos.

Only show chat from people who've agreed to be in a public screenshot, such as friends or your own alts. Other players' names and messages stay out of the listing.
